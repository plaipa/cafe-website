Manifesto — Issue 01 — Autumn

You should see what you drink.

Strata is Latin for “layers”. We chose the name because we think every good cup is made of layers that each have a job, and that you have the right to see all of them.

01 Clarity

Our glasses are glass, not ceramic. Not for looks, but so that nothing hides behind the wall. If there’s too much milk, you’ll see it. If the espresso is short, you’ll see it. We want you to.

02 Numbers

Every drink has a written recipe: grams, seconds, degrees. Our baristas don’t guess, they measure, so that today’s cup is yesterday’s cup, and tomorrow’s too.

We open the cup, not for show, but out of respect.

03 Patience

Layers aren’t built in a hurry. We pour the milk slowly and wait for the crema to settle. STRATA isn’t a place for fast coffee; it’s a place for coffee worth looking at.

04 Tabriz

A city whose bazaar was built in layers: vault on vault, century on century. STRATA sits in an alley off Abresan Street, with one long stone counter, twenty-four seats and a window onto the street.
