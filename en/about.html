Where we come from

Three things that made us

  1. 01 — The bazaar

    Under brick vaults

    Tabriz’s historic bazaar, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, shapes our space: brick, light falling from the roof, and the low murmur of conversation.

  2. 02 — The bean

    From farm to grinder

    We choose our beans with care and roast them in small batches, so there’s always something fresh on the bar.

  3. 03 — The people

    Tables for staying

    Café Jaan is for students, artists, bazaar merchants and anyone looking for a quiet hour in a busy day.

Coffee is the excuse. People are the jaan.
