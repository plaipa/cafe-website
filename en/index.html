Section 02 — The extraction

Hot water is pushed through freshly ground coffee at 9 bar, pulling out the bean’s strength and aroma in about 25–30 seconds.

  • 9bar of pressure
  • 90–96°water temperature
  • 25–30second extraction

Section 03 — The reward

Caffeine peaks 30–60 minutes later and takes the place of adenosine, the tiredness signal, on your brain’s receptors. That is the power that travels from the cup to you.

  • 30–60minutes to peak
  • ≈ 5hour half-life
  • 400mg daily limit (FDA)
