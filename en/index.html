01 — The bean

It all begins with a seed

A coffee bean is really the seed of a red cherry. Roasting awakens hundreds of aromatic compounds inside it, the scent that promises a good morning before the first sip.

02 — Aroma

Before the first sip, the scent wakes you

Steam, warmth and aroma: a simple ritual that starts the day for so many of us. The moment the cup is set in front of you, the mood of the day begins to shift.

03 — The molecule

The power of coffee fits in one molecule

Caffeine, C₈H₁₀N₄O₂: twenty-four atoms of carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen and oxygen. This tiny molecule is what travels from the cup into you.

04 — The spark

Caffeine takes fatigue’s seat

All day long a molecule called adenosine settles onto receptors in your brain and says “I’m tired.” Caffeine is shaped much like it, so it takes those seats and turns the volume of fatigue down.

  • 30–60minutes to peak effect
  • ≈ 5hour half-life in healthy adults
  • 400mg a day: upper limit for most healthy adults (FDA)

05 — You

The power of coffee is this spark.

Coffee isn’t magic. It’s good company. The rest is up to you: energy, focus, and a moment you keep for yourself.
