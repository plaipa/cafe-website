02 Espresso & crema

Twenty-eight seconds. The base of everything.

18 g of freshly ground coffee, water at 93° pushed through at 9 bar. In 28 seconds, 36 g of espresso drips out, and a golden crema settles on top.

03 Milk

Milk, to 65° and not a degree more.

Above 65°C the sugars in milk scorch and its sweetness is gone. We steam with a thermometer, not by guesswork, and pour slowly over the espresso so the layers stay apart.

65°C
Volume

04 Foam

Eight millimetres, and a heart.

Microfoam means bubbles too small to see. That thin layer is what makes the drawing on top possible.
