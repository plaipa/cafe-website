Every cup is layered.

Look closely at a STRATA iced latte: espresso, ice, cold milk, and the foam that settles last of all.

Open the cup The anatomical menu

01 Espresso

It all starts with 36 grams

18 g of freshly ground coffee, water at 93° pushed through at 9 bar. In 28 seconds, 36 g of espresso drips over the ice: a dark base for every layer that follows.

02 Ice & milk

Cold milk, slowly, over the ice.

We pour the milk over the ice so it slows down and rests on the espresso instead of mixing into it. That is how the layers stay apart, and why you can see where they meet.

Temperature
4°C
Volume

03 Foam

And last, the lightest layer.

Cold foam settles on top of everything. Every sip passes through it: first the softness of milk, then the bite of espresso.

15ml
Depth

04 Anatomy

Now see them apart.

Five parts, each with its own number and reason. That is all an iced latte is; no more, no less.
